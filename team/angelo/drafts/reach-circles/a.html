Altitude is not reach

A draft for Angelo. The rings are reach: what a thing can touch, from thinking alone at the centre to acting as a person at the edge. The walls between them are the barriers that hold it in. The altitudes of execution are not rings; they are markers placed on the target. Tap a ring or a marker.

The one idea

Altitude says how the work is organised. Reach says what it can touch.

Going up an altitude adds structure: a loop adds tools and steps, an agent adds memory and its own wake, a swarm adds others to coordinate. None of that moves a wall. Reach moves only when someone grants a tool, a network host, a credential, a connector or a schedule.

Altitudes on the target: A0 compute, A1 LLM call, A2 loop, A3 agent, A4 swarm.

Altitude is how the work is organised: one call, a loop, an agent with memory and a wake, a swarm of agents.

Reach is what it can touch: set by the walls around it (tools, sandbox, network, credentials, connectors, approval), not by its altitude.

Climbing from a call to a loop usually widens reach because tools are handed over; climbing from an agent to a swarm adds coordination, not reach.

The risk is reach larger than the mandate needs: a low altitude holding a high reach, like a one-line question asked in a chat that holds an agent's connectors.
