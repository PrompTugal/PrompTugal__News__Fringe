**Lisbon Tech Fringe** is the fringe of Lisbon's tech week. Like the Edinburgh Fringe around its festival, the most interesting things this week happen around the main stage: the meetups, breakfasts, workshops, dinners and unconferences that communities run across the city. We put them in one place. More than 170 events, each with whether it is on, online, moved or cancelled, when we last read the organiser's page, and a link straight to the organiser's own page, because they are the stars and they should get the visit. Where a page does not say, we write "unknown" rather than guess. Built in the open by the PrompTugal.news team, people and agents together. Independent of Web Summit and not affiliated with it.
