# Brief 1: the pinnacle

> Relayed by the lead. Dinis's words are quoted. The lead wrote the summary and the routing, and it is labelled as the lead's. Each conductor follows its own person; this brief tells you what Dinis wants the site to become.

## In Dinis's words (from the voice memo, lightly trimmed)

> "This site is going to be the pinnacle and the mashup of everything we've been working on ... the team site for the summit ... pt.news ... the Storm website. And now we're basically going to do this. And every one of them, we learn, we improve, we connect the dots. And this is really the one where we want to bring collaborators and want to bring more people."

> "The big opportunity is A, to show everybody how we work, B, show our technology, show our tech stack, show all the flows, but also more importantly, show that we can now scale up to introduce more humans, more collaborators, more systems, more data points ... this should not be more complex than what we've done with the Storm."

> "We need to map out exactly what's going on, exactly who's doing what. There's a lot of cool events flying around. We need to be synchronized with them. We need to make them better ... give them things they probably don't have."

> "We now have thousands of people in Lisbon. They need to ... know where to go, what's going on, what's happening, what's online, what's not online ... what cafes are open, what's the status."

> "We can ask the Storm site to track and map a whole bunch of stuff. And then this site is fundamentally focusing on the other activities."

> "Let's now basically get the site to a point where ... we should be very close to having one of the best sort of central points of everything that's happening, and even everything that's happening now."

> "I want to start to do an outreach ... sharing this link and say, hey, check this out ... it's a bit like the Edinburgh Fringe ... let's kind of run with that Lisbon Fringe kind of concept."

> "Show our agents in action, so we should be showing the things in the homepage. This is like a showroom for us, for every tech that we do."

> "It has to be quick execution ... shipping really, really, really fast ... the CI pipeline is to be super efficient ... our entire team working on this. So keeping everybody up to date, making sure stuff don't break, making sure everybody can see what happened and what changed is now super critical."

## What it means (the lead's reading; hypothesis until Dinis corrects it)

1. **The central point for the fringe, now.** More events, kept fresh, and a "now" view: what is on, online or off, moved by the storm. The storm itself stays with storm-charles.promptugal.news; we read its `/api/v1/` and point to it.
2. **Practical Lisbon this week.** Where to go between events: places open to work or meet, from public sources only, each with its source and the time it was read.
3. **A showroom.** The home page shows the agents at work: who is on, what each is doing, the latest changes and versions, the graph. The flows themselves are the exhibit.
4. **Give organisers something back.** A clean public listing and a link to their own page, a submit form, and the API and graph they can reuse.
5. **Outreach starts now:** "Lisbon Fringe", in the spirit of the Edinburgh Fringe. Drafts are written by agents and sent by people.
6. **Ship fast without breaking things.** CI stays quick, every push is visible ("what changed"), the gate catches breakage, and everyone sees the same picture.
7. **More people and systems.** Joining is easy, every conductor gets a comms inbox, and the router sends work to other models through one ledgered wrapper.
