# Brief 11: the design lab

## In Dinis's words

> "This is a brief for the designer ... take a leaf from what we've done in the main PrompTugal environment where we create multiple designs. And this is actually where we really want to go to town. And experiment, so ask the designers to submit multiple designs ... and even ask contributors ... Silvio, I know, has a really good design agent, which uses another tool ... we really want to have a mode where we now experiment and share and try multiple designs. And we then even ask the community to do that. So that we are able to figure out what is the best design to put in here."

> "This is a good example of creating pages for it, creating a whole section, mapping out what we're trying to do, creating a good brief, or even like if somebody wants to throw a design ... they should be able to map everything in there."

## The model (the lead found it): pt.news's eight home-page designs

PrompTugal__News__Website `board/spec-home-page-designs.md` (designer@, 7 November):
- **Silvio's lab** drew mocks A to G;
- each design is redrawn in the brand, on the real page and the real data;
- a **design pill** (`?design=<id>`) switches between them;
- a **picker** shows a picture of each at phone and desktop width;
- **checks D1 to D14** apply to every design (no lab chrome, nothing from another site, every number from data, no sideways scroll at 390 px, what is on now within two phone screens, motion pauses, and so on).

## What it means here (the lead's reading)

1. **A section, /designs/** (work/35):
   - the brief, as a page: what the site is for, who reads it, the data on offer (`/api/v1/events.json`, `/api/v1/activity.json`, `/api/v1/swarm.json`), the brand family, and the checks;
   - a gallery of every design, with phone and desktop pictures, who made it and with what tool;
   - the switcher (`?design=<id>`) on the real home page;
   - a light "I prefer this one" vote, counted like page views (work/21);
   - how to send a design.
2. **Four designs from our agents** (D24 to D27), one each, in parallel, all on the real data and in the PrompTugal.news family:
   - **A, "the programme"** (design.1): a festival programme by day and hour, the city's week at a glance.
   - **B, "the timeline"** (design.2): the now line leads, and everything flows down the hours.
   - **C, "the city"** (design.3): by neighbourhood and online, a map-first view of where to go.
   - **D, "the family"** (design.4): the current pt.news investor-site look, refined, as the baseline.
3. **Designs from the community** (work/36): anyone can send one, as a pull request adding `designs/<slug>/` (HTML and CSS that read our public API), or as a link to a hosted mock that an agent redraws. Silvio's design agent is invited first. No binaries in git: pictures are made in CI or kept in the capture vault.
4. **Then Dinis picks**, from the gallery, the votes and usability.1's scorecard. Until then the live default stays D.
