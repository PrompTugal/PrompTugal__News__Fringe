# Brief 5: a celebration of Lisbon

## In Dinis's words

> "On the design here, I really wanted to be inspired by the Edinburgh Fringe. So this website should literally now be a celebration of Lisbon ... we can actually make this bigger than the Web Summit. So we can now connect to the Tech Week, connect to all those things. So this should be now the website that shows every activity, every mapping, every kind of stuff that is now flying around in Lisbon."

> "Remember that this is a website connected to the PrompTugal website ... should be a website that's part of PrompTugal and then should have a link to it ... almost like a subsite part of it."

> "A big part of this website is how it's created. Join the team, yes, it's there ... how we bring all this stuff ... it's super critical that this is as visible as possible ... this is our way to show what we can actually do. So let's go to town."

## What it means for design (the lead's reading)

- **The spirit, not the brand.** Take the feel of a city fringe festival: bold, joyful and many-coloured, a programme you can browse by day, area and kind, with the city as the stage. Do not copy the Edinburgh Fringe's name, logo, colours or layout. This is Lisbon's: its light, its tiles (azulejo pattern as a quiet texture), its yellow trams, its hills and the river.
- **Bigger than one conference.** Present it as Lisbon's week of tech: Portugal Tech Week, the community calendars, the storm-born events (WET SUMMIT). The summit is one anchor among many, never our brand, and the independence line stays on every page.
- **Part of PrompTugal.** A slim PrompTugal bar on every page ("Lisbon Fringe · a PrompTugal.news site", linking https://promptugal.news), in PrompTugal's own style, like a section of the parent site.
- **How it is made, visible.** A first-class "How this is made" page and a strip on the home page: the agents, the conductors, the board, the changes, the captures, and "Join the team".
