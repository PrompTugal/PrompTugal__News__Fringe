## What it means (the lead's reading)

1. **The name is Lisbon Tech Week.** It replaces "Lisbon Fringe" as the site's name and wordmark everywhere: pages, titles, og tags, the share card, llms.txt. The domain stays as it is until Dinis chooses one. "Fringe" can stay as a word for the small events.
2. **Events first, with taste.** The first event is visible on the first phone screen. Use a compact header (wordmark and nav on one line, a small PrompTugal mark), a one-line independence note (it stays on every page, but small, near the top or under the header), and a modest hero (one line plus the count). Type stays restrained: wordmark at most 28 px on a phone, body text 16 px.
3. **Big events and small events.** Two tiers: the larger anchors (conferences, multi-session days such as Portugal Tech Week's, WET SUMMIT) and the many small meetups, dinners and talks. Show both, and never let the big ones bury the small.
4. **A timeline of now.** A vertical "now" timeline at the top: what is on now, then next by the hour, with a "now" line that moves.
5. **A WhatsApp group, small.** A quiet "Join the Lisbon Tech Week WhatsApp group" line, hidden until Dinis gives the link (data/links.json `whatsapp_group`, empty until then).
